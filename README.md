### 📊 8. EDA Visualizations and Key Findings


The following visualizations were created using Matplotlib and Seaborn.


#### 1. Gender-wise Purchase Distribution
   
Male customers contributed approximately 46.2% of recorded purchases.
Female customers contributed approximately 45.1%.
Unknown gender accounted for approximately 8.6%.

Insight: Male and female customers showed similar purchase contributions.


#### 2. Purchase Distribution by Age Group

Customers aged 45–60 contributed the highest total purchase amount.
The 0–18 age group contributed the lowest total purchase amount.


Insight: The 45–60 age group represents an important segment for further customer behavior analysis.

#### 3. Monthly Purchase Trends

Prominent purchase increases were observed in March 2025 and July 2025.
Purchase amounts fluctuated during the analyzed period.
October 2025 showed a substantial decrease compared with September.

Insight: Monthly fluctuations should be investigated to understand customer activity, promotional periods, and reporting completeness.


#### 4. Correlation Analysis

Age vs. Purchase Amount: Approximately 0.02 correlation.
Feedback Score vs. Purchase Amount: Close to zero correlation.

Insight: Age and feedback score showed almost no linear relationship with purchase amount.

#### 5. Data Quality Analysis

The analysis identified:

Invalid age values such as -10 and 250.
1,009 missing Last Purchase Date values.
Four negative purchase amounts.
An unusually high purchase amount of 9,999,999.

Insight: Additional data validation is important to improve the reliability of business reporting.
